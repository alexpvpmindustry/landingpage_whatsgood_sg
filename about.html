About Whatsgood.sg

Singapore's most trusted platform for discovering exceptional restaurants, services, and products

Our Story: Born from a Simple Question

"Where should I actually eat in Singapore?"

This simple question, asked by countless residents and visitors daily, sparked the creation of Whatsgood.sg. Founded in 2025 by a team of passionate Singaporeans who were frustrated with generic reviews and tourist-focused recommendations, we set out to create something different – a platform that actually helps locals discover what's truly good.

Too often, we found ourselves wading through hundreds of reviews, unable to distinguish between genuine recommendations and paid promotions. We realized Singapore needed a trusted voice – a platform where every recommendation is earned through rigorous evaluation and personal experience.

Our Rigorous 15-Point Evaluation Process

Unlike other review platforms, we don't rely solely on user reviews. Our expert team personally evaluates every business using our comprehensive 15-point criteria:

Quality Assessment (5 Points)

  • Product/Service Excellence
  • Consistency Across Visits
  • Attention to Detail
  • Innovation & Uniqueness
  • Overall Standards

Value & Service (5 Points)

  • Value for Money
  • Customer Service Quality
  • Wait Times & Efficiency
  • Problem Resolution
  • Staff Knowledge & Expertise

Experience & Trust (5 Points)

  • Ambiance & Environment
  • Cleanliness & Hygiene
  • Accessibility & Location
  • Customer Satisfaction History
  • Business Integrity & Ethics

Meet Our Expert Team

Our review team consists of passionate Singaporeans with decades of combined experience in food, education, lifestyle, and business evaluation. Each team member brings unique expertise and a commitment to helping fellow Singaporeans make better choices.

🍽️ Culinary Experts

Former F&B professionals and passionate food enthusiasts with deep knowledge of Singapore's dining scene across all price points and cuisines.

🎓 Education Specialists

Experienced educators and parents who understand Singapore's education system and the challenges faced by both local and international students.

🏠 Lifestyle Consultants

Service industry veterans who know what makes a business truly exceptional and worth recommending to friends and family.

Our Commitment to Independence

We never accept payment for rankings or reviews. Our recommendations are based solely on merit, ensuring you receive honest, unbiased guidance. When we do feature partnerships (like with Golden Tuition Academy), it's because they genuinely represent the best in their category – not because they paid us.

🚫 No Paid Rankings

Every position is earned through our evaluation process

👁️ Anonymous Reviews

Businesses don't know we're evaluating them

🔄 Regular Re-evaluation

We revisit businesses to ensure consistent quality

📱 Community Feedback

We listen to our community and adjust recommendations accordingly

Looking Forward: Our Vision for Singapore

We envision a Singapore where every meal is memorable, every service exceeds expectations, and every recommendation you receive is trustworthy. By elevating the best businesses and helping consumers make informed choices, we're contributing to a culture of excellence that benefits everyone.
